About the Project

We are introducing a community-powered, reward-based crowdfunding initiative with a vision to establish a beautiful gift store offering personalised gifts, premium gift boxes, hampers, and gifting solutions for every special occasion.

Instead of relying entirely on traditional investors, our aim is to bring together a community of supporters who can participate in building this business from the ground up.

🎁 Our Proposed Lifetime Annual Voucher Program

We aim to introduce a special appreciation program for everyone who contributes towards establishing our gift store.

Here's how the proposed reward system would work:

  1. Supporters contribute an amount of their choice, starting from ₹100.
  2. Once the gift store successfully opens, eligible supporters would receive a shopping voucher equivalent to their contribution amount.
  3. A fresh voucher of the same value is proposed to be issued every year.
  4. Our long-term vision is to continue this annual renewal throughout each eligible supporter's lifetime.
  5. Vouchers would be redeemable against eligible purchases from our gift store.

Example: A supporter contributing ₹100 could receive a ₹100 shopping voucher every year under the proposed program, subject to the final terms.

The voucher would be a shopping benefit, not a cash repayment, interest payment, or financial investment return.

📌 Important Conditions & Transparency

  • Store opening: The business will be established only if sufficient funding is raised and the project becomes financially and operationally feasible.
  • Reward eligibility: The proposed annual voucher program will begin only after the store successfully opens and the program is formally launched.
  • Lifetime renewal: Annual lifetime vouchers are a proposed benefit, not a guaranteed entitlement. Implementation depends on financial sustainability, legal feasibility, and final published terms.
  • Voucher usage: Vouchers would be usable against eligible purchases and would not be exchangeable for cash.
  • No ownership: Contributions will not provide equity, partnership rights, profit-sharing, interest, or dividends.
  • Project risk: The store may be delayed or may never open. In that situation, the proposed rewards may not become available.
  • Refunds: Contributions are intended to be non-refundable where legally permissible. The final refund policy must comply with applicable Indian law.
  • Final terms: Voucher validity, redemption rules, renewal conditions, eligibility, and other details will be published before contributions are accepted.

This proposal does not constitute a guaranteed offer of lifetime gifts, a financial investment, or a promise of monetary returns. The campaign structure and terms are subject to legal review before fundraising begins.
